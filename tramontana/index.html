TRAMONTANA

An old wooden schooner, a coast of lemon terraces and painted towns, and islands out on the blue.

Carry lemons and limoncello, capers and wine from harbour to harbour. Mostly, sail.

Helm: drag bottom left, let go to hold course.
Sheet: the slider above it. Watch the telltales.
Sails: lever bottom right. Reef when she heels too far.
Come into harbour slowly. Furl the sails early, come alongside the yellow kerb, lines ashore.
Keys: A/D helm, W/S sails, Z/X sheet, V vane, N anchor, Enter lines ashore, M chart.
