LIBERTALIA

The Empire holds the islands in chains. One free harbour remains, hidden on Cayo Escondido.

Learn the wind. Take their ships. Free the people they carry.

Helm: drag bottom left, let go to hold course.
Sails: lever bottom right.
You cannot sail closer than about 45 degrees to the wind.
Come alongside a ship slowly to board her.
Keys: A/D helm, W/S sails, Q/E fire, B board, M chart.
INFO (top right) explains sailing, fighting and freeing the islands.
