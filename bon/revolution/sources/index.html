How to read the strip

The strip runs from stone tools (left) to the present (right). Time is on a log scale: each factor of ten in years before 2026 gets the same length, so 10 to 100 years ago (1926–2016) is as long as 100,000 to 1 million years ago. Read the year off the scale at the top or bottom edge.

Each node states what changed, who gained and who paid. Where the strip is crowded, a node is printed further right; a short mark at the top of its band shows its true date, and a thin line leads to the text. A short legend is repeated about every 60 cm.

Needle. Stick a needle into the strip where you think society took a wrong turn. Note the three-digit node number next to it, or the year on the scale.

Open points from the fact check

  • 18 nodes are marked ? (contested or estimate). The mark is printed on the strip.
  • In the bibliography, elements marked † were not re-checked online. Entries marked [UNVERIFIED] depend on the edition used (Gandhi, Engels first edition).
  • Bairoch's manufacturing shares for 1900 (India ~2 %, China ~6 %) are not confirmed; table values exist for 1880 and 1913 only. They are not used on the strip.
  • Kaczynski's bombs killed 3 people; the number injured is given as 23 (Britannica) or 24 (FBI).
  • Dartmouth: the term "artificial intelligence" dates from the 1955 proposal; the workshop took place in 1956.
  • Anna Göldi (executed 1782 in Glarus) was formally convicted of poisoning, not witchcraft.
